# Trigger prompts

Given only the skills' `name` and `description`, an agent should load `fire-os-accessibility` for every prompt in the first list and not for any in the second.

## Should trigger

1. VoiceView reads my Fire TV app's buttons in a weird order, how do I fix it?
2. TextToSpeech is silent on my Fire TV Stick but works on my phone.
3. How do I duck ExoPlayer audio under audio description on Fire TV?
4. My Kotlin Fire TV app needs spoken subtitles. Where do I start?
5. Can my Fire OS app use the Alexa remote microphone for voice commands?
6. Which remote buttons does VoiceView reserve on Fire TV?
7. Add usage hints for VoiceView to my Android TV RecyclerView on Fire TV.
8. Does my Fire TV player meet EN 301 549 for captions and audio description?
9. How do I check from adb whether VoiceView spoke during my app's playback?
10. Our Compose for TV app narrates itself and VoiceView keeps talking over it.

## Should not trigger

1. Set up focus management with TVFocusGuideView in my React Native tvOS app.
2. Migrate my Vega OS app to a React Native monorepo.
3. Make my iOS app work with VoiceOver.
4. Add TalkBack support to my Android phone app.
5. Write an Alexa skill that answers trivia questions.
6. Build a Kepler / Vega app's audio playback with keplerscript-audio-lib.
7. Improve the color contrast of my web app for WCAG AA.
8. Fix a Gradle build error in my Android phone app.
9. Normalize the loudness of a podcast episode with ffmpeg.
10. Style swimlanes and hero banners on Android TV in React Native.
